LogLog — the geometric mean

One bucket per item. Each bucket remembers its largest rank. The estimate is α · m · 2^(mean register), and α ≈ 0.39701 is the bias constant from the asymptotic analysis.

SECTION 1 · THE UPDATE RULE

One bucket per item

For each item: hash → take the top log2(m) bits as bucket → write the max rank into that bucket.

hash → bucket = top(log2(m)) bits → register[bucket] = max(register[bucket], rank) → estimate = α · m · 2^(mean(register))

In the educational implementation (scripts/03-loglog.py): bucket id from the top k bits, rank from leading zeros, register = max.

SECTION 2 · THE ESTIMATE

estimate = α · m · 2^(mean register)

For every register equal to r, the estimate is simply:

estimate = α · m · 2^r
estimate = 0.39701 × 64 × 2^5 = 8128.13

α ≈ 0.39701 is the LogLog bias constant. It is the asymptotic constant from the theory — not fitted to a dataset.

SECTION 3 · WHERE THE BIAS CONSTANT COMES FROM

α ≈ 0.39701 — derived, not guessed

The estimate formula has an asymptotic constant α. For FM that constant came out as φ ≈ 0.77351 via Theorem 3.A (the product from the probability analysis). For LogLog the same kind of asymptotic analysis produces α ≈ 0.39701.

The mathematical route is parallel: exact distribution of the max → smooth approximation → Mellin-type transform → constant. The paper's Theorem 3.A gives the φ product; LogLog's derivation gives the α constant. Same family, different estimator shape.

Relation to your code: LogLog.alpha = 0.39701, estimate = alpha * m * (2 ** avg_register), then rounded.
